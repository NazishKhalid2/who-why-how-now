# Remove “vibe-coded” patterns and enforce launch safeguards

## What will change
- Remove all fabricated testimonials, customer names, ratings, installation counts, founding dates, certifications, response-time promises, and other claims not supplied by the business.
- Replace vague or generated marketing copy with direct, factual language based only on the company details and services already provided.
- Remove pill-shaped buttons, decorative glow blobs, gradient text, exaggerated hover motion, and page-wide scroll reveal effects. Keep the supplied white-and-water-blue direction, restrained transitions, square-soft controls, and clear layouts.
- Remove locally generated stock-style photos from customer-facing pages. Use only the company-supplied logo, product image, and blog infographics; where no verified photo exists, use clean typography and icons instead.
- Remove placeholder social links, the nonfunctional language selector, and any other interface that suggests unavailable functionality.
- Replace em dashes and en dashes in visible copy with ordinary punctuation.

## Quality and launch checks
- Check every route for layout, links, metadata, mobile/desktop rendering, and browser errors.
- Confirm the favicon, Privacy Policy, and Terms and Conditions pages remain present and linked.
- Keep the site unpublished. Custom domain connection and removal of the “Made with Lovable” badge remain mandatory launch blockers until completed in project settings.

## Technical details
- Simplify the global visual utilities and reveal component instead of adding more animation.
- Preserve semantic color tokens and accessible icon-based controls.
- Remove unused testimonial data and generated image imports/files after all references are gone.
- Add complete social metadata to every content route while retaining its unique title and description.
